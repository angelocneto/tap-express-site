About TAP Express

25 years connecting inland cities in the shortest possible time.

TAP started in 2001 in Presidente Prudente and today delivers to 104 locations from 20 branches and bases. Own fleet, certified partners and people who know every road in the region.

How it all began

Paulo Barreto founded TAP Transportes in 2001 in Presidente Prudente with a simple idea: fast, reliable road transport for people and businesses in western São Paulo state. The first trucks bridged the region and the rest of the state.

Operations grew into northern Paraná and Mato Grosso do Sul. Today distribution leaves headquarters in Regente Feijó, on the Raposo Tavares highway, and reaches the network's 20 branches and bases, each responsible for a group of cities in its region.

Mission, vision and values

Mission: improve processes and technology every year and deliver what was agreed with the customer.

Vision: grow sustainably, connecting the longest distances in the shortest possible time while respecting the environment.

  • Innovation: intelligent route planner, smart route tracking for shippers and the TAPIA assistant.
  • Speed with safety: delivery within 18 hours between network cities.
  • Human capital: local teams who know customers by name.
  • Social and environmental responsibility in operations and in the choice of partners.

How the network works

Each branch serves neighbouring cities on fixed routes. Smaller towns have set service days (for example Tuesday and Thursday), which keeps delivery times predictable and costs low. See the full list under cities served or explore the network map.

Frequently asked questions

How long has TAP Express been around?

TAP Transportes was founded in 2001 in Presidente Prudente by Paulo Barreto. That is 25 years on the road.

Where is the headquarters?

On highway SP 270 Raposo Tavares, km 556, in Regente Feijó, in the Presidente Prudente region. Distribution to the whole network starts there.

How many branches does TAP have?

20 branches and bases in São Paulo, Paraná and Mato Grosso do Sul, serving 104 locations.

Does TAP ship outside these regions?

The network covers western São Paulo state, northern Paraná and Mato Grosso do Sul. For other destinations, call the central office at +55 18 3918-7777 and the team will assess case by case.
