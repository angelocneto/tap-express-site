Who we are

Regional logistics with clarity, coordination and proximity.

TAP Transportes started in 2001 in Presidente Prudente, founded by Paulo Barreto to provide fast, well-executed road transport for people and businesses across the region.

Today distribution runs from Presidente Prudente and 20 branches and bases in western São Paulo state, northern Paraná and Mato Grosso do Sul, serving 104 locations with daily routes and fixed service days in smaller towns.

  • Innovation: intelligent route planner and TAPIA in daily operations
  • Speed with safety in every delivery
  • Human capital and social responsibility
  • Sustainability and respect for the environment

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers from the TAP team.

Which cities does TAP serve?

104 locations across São Paulo, Paraná and Mato Grosso do Sul. The full list, with the responsible branch and route days, is under cities served. You can also type your city into the network map search.

What is the delivery time?

Up to 18 hours for the cities we serve. Some smaller towns have fixed delivery days, for example Tuesday and Thursday. Those days are shown next to each city under cities served.

How do I track my parcel?

Right here on the website, under Tracking: enter the shipper's CNPJ or CPF, the invoice number and the tracking password provided by TAP. It is always those three details. TAPIA, in the corner of the screen, can also take you there.

I need to change who pays the freight. Is that possible?

Only while the freight has not yet been paid. In that case the current payer must file a disagreement statement within the period allowed by SEFAZ. After payment the change is no longer possible. Contact the branch that issued the CT-e for guidance.

How do I get a job at TAP?

Open positions are listed under Work with us, where you send your CV straight to our candidate center. If you prefer, send it to rh@taptransportes.com.br.

Does TAP do house moves?

No. We handle parcels, pouches and LTL freight between network cities. We do not do residential or commercial moves.
